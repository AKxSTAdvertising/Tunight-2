FAQ

Good to know.

Where is TUNIGHT?

We are at J-1, Opposite Gaur Central Mall, RDC, Raj Nagar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201002, right opposite Gaur Central Mall in Raj Nagar.

Is the food vegetarian?

Yes. Our kitchen serves vegetarian food, from tandoor favourites like paneer tikka and malai chaap to momos, starters and platters.

How many guests can the banquet hall host?

The Grand Banquet Hall is suited to 100 to 150 guests, ideal for birthdays, farewells, family functions and corporate events.

How do I reserve a table?

Book through Zomato or Swiggy Dineout, call us on +91 78400 03009, or send your details on WhatsApp using the reservation form on this site. Reservations are subject to confirmation.

Can you host private and corporate events?

Yes. Tell us the date, guest count and occasion on WhatsApp or call, and we will share the options for your event.
